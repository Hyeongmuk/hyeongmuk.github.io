These are working notes on two-dimensional conformal field theory, built from axioms stated explicitly in the text: locality, Möbius covariance, and a convergent operator product expansion. Every claim is either proved in the notes, stated as an axiom, or logged with its source and where it is used.

## Contents

1. The plane: the conformal algebra and conformal group, and the structure of a 2D CFT.
2. The sphere: correlation functions, radial quantization, Virasoro representations, conformal blocks and minimal models, and crossing symmetry.
3. The cylinder and the torus: theta functions, the free boson, the free fermion and the Ising model, characters of minimal models, the $S$-matrix and the Verlinde formula, and modular invariant partition functions.
4. The disk and the annulus: conformal boundary conditions, boundary states, and the $g$-function.
5. Higher genus: cutting along a circle, the factorization hypothesis, and counting conformal blocks.
6. Appendix: mathematical formulas, the axioms at a glance, and results used without proof.
