These notes trace a route from derived algebraic geometry and stable homotopy theory to chromatic homotopy theory and its applications to symmetry TFTs and invertible phases of matter. The algebraic geometry chapter is developed in detail; the later chapters are an outline of goals toward the chromatic-height picture of symmetry, carried out where the notes currently stand.

## Contents

1. Algebraic geometry: derived self-intersections (the Koszul resolution, Serre's intersection formula, the cotangent complex), loop spaces and Hochschild homology, and planned sections on derived categories and noncommutative geometry, derived critical loci and the BV formalism, and flat directions and gauge symmetries.
2. Algebraic topology: the Pontryagin–Thom construction and Lie framings, with planned sections on Steenrod squares and the Adams spectral sequence, the image of $J$ and the $e$-invariant, and Spin and Pin bordism.
3. Chromatic homotopy (planned): complex orientations and formal group laws, and Morava $K$-theory of $B\mathbb{Z}/p$.
4. Physics: SymTFT and invertible phases (planned): Dijkgraaf–Witten theory and chromatic height, and Anderson duality and the classification of invertible phases.
