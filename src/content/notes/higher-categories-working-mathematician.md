These study notes were taken at the summer school *Higher Categories for the Working Mathematician* at the Mathematisches Institut, Georg-August-Universität Göttingen, and written up by **Hyeongmuk Lim** (July 27, 2026).

The notes follow four lectures, from why $(\infty,1)$-categories are needed, through higher categories in action and stable $\infty$-categories, to higher principal bundles and connections.

## Contents

1. $(\infty,1)$-categories and beyond: motivation, examples and $(\infty,n)$-categories, complicial sets.
2. Higher categories in action: $\infty$-operads and their algebras, factorization algebras and $\mathbb{E}_n$-algebras, spans, the Gray–Boardman–Vogt tensor product, algebras in spans and 2-Segal objects.
3. Introduction to stable $\infty$-categories and spectra: commutative monoids and groups, (semi)additive $\infty$-categories, delooping, spectra and homology theories, tensor products.
4. Higher principal bundles and connections: higher groups, higher principal bundles, higher Lie algebras, connections and adjustments, applications.
