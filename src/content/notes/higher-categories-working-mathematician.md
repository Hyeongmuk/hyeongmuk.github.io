These study notes were taken at the summer school *Higher Categories for the Working Mathematician* at the Mathematisches Institut, Georg-August-Universität Göttingen, and written up by **Hyeongmuk Lim** (July 27, 2026). The lectures were given by **Viktoriya Ozornova**, **Claudia Scheimbauer** and **Walker Stern**, **Rune Haugseng**, and **Christian Saemann**.

The notes follow four lectures, from why $(\infty,1)$-categories are needed, through higher categories in action and stable $\infty$-categories, to higher principal bundles and connections.

## Contents

1. $(\infty,1)$-categories and beyond (Viktoriya Ozornova): motivation, examples and $(\infty,n)$-categories, complicial sets.
2. $(\infty,n)$-categories in action (Claudia Scheimbauer and Walker Stern): $\infty$-operads and their algebras, factorization algebras and $\mathbb{E}_n$-algebras, spans, the Gray–Boardman–Vogt tensor product, algebras in spans and 2-Segal objects.
3. An introduction to stable $\infty$-categories and spectra (Rune Haugseng): commutative monoids and groups, (semi)additive $\infty$-categories, delooping, spectra and homology theories, tensor products.
4. An introduction to higher principal bundles and their applications (Christian Saemann): higher groups, higher principal bundles, higher Lie algebras, connections and adjustments, applications.
