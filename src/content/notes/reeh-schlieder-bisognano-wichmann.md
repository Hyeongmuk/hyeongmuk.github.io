The Reeh–Schlieder and Bisognano–Wichmann theorems reveal deep structures in the vacuum entanglement of a quantum field theory formulated in terms of Wightman axioms. This note discusses the content and proof of the two theorems.

## Contents

1. The Reeh–Schlieder theorem: the Wightman axioms, implications of the spectrum condition, and the theorem.
2. Introduction to modular theory: the finite-dimensional model and the Tomita–Takesaki theorem.
3. The Bisognano–Wichmann theorem: statement and the Euclidean path integral argument.
