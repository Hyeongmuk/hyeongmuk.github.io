The Källén–Lehmann and Nambu–Goldstone theorems are two non-perturbative theorems that apply to all quantum field theories formalized in terms of Wightman axioms. This note reviews the shared ideas behind the two theorems and discusses their proofs.

## Contents

1. Introduction: positivity, the spectrum condition, symmetry, and locality as the shared ingredients.
2. Wightman axioms.
3. The Källén–Lehmann representation.
4. The Nambu–Goldstone theorem: conserved currents and order parameters, the theorem, examples and remarks.
