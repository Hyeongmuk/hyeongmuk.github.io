These notes organize the group-cohomology proposals for gauge anomalies and SPT phases around anomaly inflow. Sorting $(d+1)$-dimensional response functionals by whether they depend on the bundle class alone and whether their couplings are forced to be discrete separates the perturbative and global anomaly sectors, which assemble into a single differential-cohomology group. For finite $G$ only the torsion sector survives; there, gauging an SPT phase yields a Dijkgraaf–Witten theory, and twisted gauging amounts to stacking an SPT before gauging.

## Contents

1. **Anomalies and their cohomological proposals:** the response one dimension up, perturbative anomaly, global anomaly, and the differential-cohomology synthesis.
2. **SPT, gauge theory, and cohomology:** the simplicial model for gauge theory, vertex versus edge labelings, from SPT to gauge theory, and twisted gauging as stacking with an SPT.
