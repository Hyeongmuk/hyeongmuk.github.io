These notes give a short proof that every smooth cubic surface over an algebraically closed field contains exactly 27 lines, using the incidence correspondence between cubic surfaces and lines, and close by indicating what remains of the count when the surface is singular.

## Contents

1. Introduction.
2. The geometry of cubic surfaces: the 27 lines on the Fermat cubic and the incidence correspondence as a projective bundle over the Grassmannian.
3. Deformation of lines: a line on a smooth cubic surface has no first-order deformations.
4. Counting the 27 lines: finite étale projection, and the same count 27 as a top Chern class on the Grassmannian (Schubert calculus).
5. Outlook: for singular cubic surfaces, the number 27 survives as the degree of a virtual fundamental class.
