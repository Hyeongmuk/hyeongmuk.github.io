A study of the Haldane gap and constraints on the low-energy physics of quantum spin chains. The manuscript follows spin coherent-state path integrals and antiferromagnetic Heisenberg chains, develops the nonlinear sigma model and its topological term, and discusses original and generalized Lieb–Schultz–Mattis theorems.

## Contents

1. **Quantization of spin:** spin coherent states and spinor path integrals.
2. **Spin chain models:** ferromagnetic and antiferromagnetic Heisenberg chains.
3. **Renormalization group analysis:** the SO(3) nonlinear sigma model, large-N limit, and the role of the topological term.
4. **The Lieb–Schultz–Mattis theorem:** original theorem and generalizations.
