These notes introduce the categorical and diagrammatic language used to describe topological quantum field theories in three dimensions, followed by state-sum constructions.

## Contents

1. **Planar diagram algebra:** unitary fusion categories and their structure.
2. **Fusion categories and planar diagrams:** graphical calculus and consistency relations.
3. **Braiding and twist:** additional diagrammatic structures.
4. **State-sum TQFTs:** quantum dimensions and F-matrices.
5. **Examples:** Turaev–Viro and Dijkgraaf–Witten constructions.
