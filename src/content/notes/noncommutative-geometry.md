These study notes are based on a seminar talk by **Myung Jin Jeong**, with notes written by **Hyeongmuk Lim** (May 9, 2025).

The manuscript explores the relationship between geometric spaces and their algebras of functions, and develops a path toward non-commutative geometry through homological and categorical ideas.

## Contents

1. Introduction: affine spaces and non-affine spaces
2. First step toward non-commutative geometry, including Hochschild (co)homology
3. Spectra, (co)homology theories, and stabilization
4. Geometry of categories, spectral algebraic geometry, and motives
